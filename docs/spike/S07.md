# S07: Webhooks and read calls

Run on 2026-11-12 against the PayPal sandbox in session N3, on the events from the S06a run. Tools: `scripts/spike/webhook-receiver.ts` (saves each delivery), `scripts/spike/07-webhooks.ts` (summary, signature check, read-call total) and `scripts/spike/s07-helpers.ts`. Raw deliveries are in the gitignored `.spike-logs/S07/`.

## Results

- **Q7 answered: yes, every event identifies its money movement and carries `invoice_id`.**
  - Capture events (`PAYMENT.CAPTURE.COMPLETED`) carry the capture id, the capture's `invoice_id`, and the authorization and order ids in `supplementary_data.related_ids`.
  - Refund events (`PAYMENT.CAPTURE.REFUNDED`) carry the refund id, the **refunded capture's `invoice_id`**, and an `up` link to the capture. They don't carry the authorization or order id.
  - Authorization events (`CREATED`, `VOIDED`) carry the authorization id, the order's `invoice_id`, and the order through `supplementary_data`.
- **Failure events: neither arrived.** No capture was declined or denied, and the sandbox gives no way to force one on an authorization. Refused captures (HTTP 422) produced no event at all. S38 handles `DECLINED` and `DENIED` the same way, as arch 8 already says.
- **Signatures: 8 of 8 verified** through `POST /v1/notifications/verify-webhook-signature` with the S04 client's `verifyWebhookSignature`, on the raw bodies exactly as received.
- **Read calls can total an authorization's captures.** `GET /v2/payments/authorizations/{id}` links to its order, and `GET /v2/checkout/orders/{id}` lists every capture with its amount, status and `invoice_id`. For the S06a authorization: 4 captures, $95.00, two of them `REFUNDED`. This is what S45's reconciliation needs.

## Delivery, and what went wrong first

- PayPal needs a public HTTPS address. Free Cloudflare quick tunnels dropped after a few minutes each ("Tunnel not found"), and the first deliveries were lost. A free ngrok account with its one free fixed domain stayed up, so the webhook URL needed setting only once.
- **PayPal keeps undelivered events.** `GET /v1/notifications/webhooks-events` listed all 8, and `POST /v1/notifications/webhooks-events/{id}/resend` redelivered each one (HTTP 202) with fresh, valid signatures. S38 and S48 can rely on this to recover from downtime.
- Resent deliveries arrived newest first, so **a refund event can arrive before the capture it refunds**. S38 must accept events in any order, as its plan step already says.

## What this means for Kitty

- **S38 (N4):** match capture and refund events on `invoice_id`, check the capture id, store unmatched events, and accept events in any order. Verify each signature on the raw body, exactly as received.
- **S45 (I2):** reconcile with `getAuthorization` and `getOrder`, which give the full capture list.
- **S48 (N5):** the production webhook needs a stable URL, which the Render service already has.
